# Risk and positioning

Evaluate current and candidate positions before selecting targets. Survivable approach and exit routes take precedence over attack opportunities.

## Inputs

- Adjacent enemy count
- Enemies within 2–3 tiles
- Available escape tiles
- Distance to the player
- Confined terrain
- Enemy directions around candidate tiles
- Position within the center or edge of a group

Consider approach directions and escape-route width as well as enemy count.

## Rules

1. Stay out of group centers.
2. Prefer exposed enemies at the edge.
3. Intercept enemies approaching the player.
4. Disengage before encirclement.
5. Give up attacks without a safe approach and return.

Limit each turn's scan to the local area. An 8–12 tile radius is an initial candidate, assuming few active DoGS dogs; it is not a fixed requirement.
